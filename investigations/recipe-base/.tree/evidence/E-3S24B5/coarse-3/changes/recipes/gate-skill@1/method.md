
# Method

A skill-text gate. A change to a skill's text ships only when a fresh agent
runner, started from a recorded point of a hyp tree, shows a failure in 3 or
more of 5 trials with the skills as they are, and in 1 or fewer of 5 with
the changed text. You are one such runner; the gate reads your session
afterwards. Follow the skills as they are written.
